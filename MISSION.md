# Mission: Pass the Dietitians Australia DSR exams

## Why
Thaynar trained and worked as a dietitian in Brazil. She wants to work as a dietitian in Australia, which means passing the Dietetic Skills Recognition (DSR) process run by Dietitians Australia: the written MCQ exam and the oral counselling exam.

## Success looks like
- Scores 70%+ on full 120-question timed mock exams across all 19 study areas
- Answers questions that use Australian foods, units (kJ, mmol/L) and guidelines without translating from Brazilian norms
- Runs a full oral exam simulation (Task 1 assessment 20 min, Task 2 counselling 30 min, Task 3 interprofessional call 10 min) and passes every checklist item
- Has completed the Cultural Awareness assessment and submitted the DSR application

## Constraints
- English is her second language; Portuguese hints help with new terms
- Studying around work; short sessions (15-30 min) on phone or laptop
- Key date: DSR application by 1 Dec 2026 to sit the March 2027 MCQ

## Out of scope
- Advanced specialist practice beyond entry-level (new graduate) competency
- Non-Australian guidelines except for basic science
